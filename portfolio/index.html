01 / About

The short story so far

I didn't fall in love with technology through a single "aha" moment — it happened gradually, somewhere between breaking my first Python script and fixing it three hours later at 1 a.m., grinning like I'd cracked a code nobody else could see.

That curiosity never really settled down. I chase new tools before I'm asked to, take apart problems that don't concern me just to see how they tick, and treat every hackathon as an excuse to build something that didn't exist twenty-four hours earlier. Some of those builds became dashboards. Some became recommendation engines. One became a voice assistant meant to make technology feel less intimidating for senior citizens.

Along the way I picked up a few certifications, a habit of reading documentation for fun, and a quiet belief that the best way to learn a concept is to build something that forces you to actually need it. I'm not chasing a title. I'm chasing the next interesting problem — and I'm fairly certain I haven't found the best one yet.
